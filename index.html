Table setting: photo Shoot 1

Decor and food

  • Wide shot of the full table, all six courses or the main laid out with decor.
  • Top-down flat lay of the table.
  • Detail shots: place setting, candles, centrepiece, a wine glass being filled.

Table setting up: video Shoot 1

Decor and food

  • Timelapse or stop-motion from an empty table to fully set: cloth, plates, cutlery, candles, centrepiece, then the food arriving.
  • End on candles being lit and a wide reveal.
  • Use as a Reel with on-screen text: "Setting the table for Thanksgiving".

Food close-ups (lifestyle) Shoot 2

  • Hands passing plates, clinking glasses, serving from the platter.
  • Slow-motion pours: wine, gravy, sauce.
  • Steam rising, knife through the meat, cheese or cream melting.
  • Shallow depth of field with people softly blurred behind, to show it as a shared meal.
  • Filmed on the night with real guests, so it feels genuine.

Meal prep Shoot 1

  • Chef seasoning and tying the turkey, herbs being chopped, sauces simmering.
  • The turkey going into the oven and coming out golden (match cut).
  • Plating at the pass, a hand placing the final garnish.
  • Record the natural sound: sizzling, chopping, pouring.
  • Edit as a fast-cut "Behind the kitchen doors" Reel.

Decorations Shoot 1

  • Staff placing autumn decor: pumpkins, leaves, candles, table runners.
  • Before and after of the dining area.
  • Detail photos of each decor piece in warm light.

Shoot days

Both shoot days are confirmed. The shoot times are still to be confirmed with the restaurant.

Shoot 1 · Tue 24 Nov Before the dinner

Shoot in the order the food is really made, so prep footage leads naturally into the finished dishes.

  1. Decorations: film staff decorating, then before-and-after shots of the room.
  2. Meal prep: seasoning, chopping, simmering, the oven in and out, plating at the pass.
  3. Six courses: plate and shoot each course in order, with the hero moment and wine pour.
  4. Table setting up video: the timelapse from empty to fully set with food.
  5. Table setting photos: wide, flat lay and details once everything is in place.

Shoot 2 · Thu 26 Nov, Thanksgiving night Confirmed · time TBC

A lighter, documentary-style shoot during service (7 to 11 pm), so we capture real moments without getting in the way.

  1. Before guests arrive: the room lit and ready, candles, final table touches.
  2. Kitchen in service: the crew plating, the chef at the pass, dishes going out.
  3. Guests arriving: welcome moments and the room filling up.
  4. Food close-ups (lifestyle): plates landing, passing dishes, wine pours, glasses clinking.
  5. Crew and moments: the team behind the night, smiles, behind-the-scenes.
Tips: film everything vertical (9:16) first, then take horizontal stills. Keep 3 to 5 seconds of each action. Make sure the kitchen is tidy and uniforms are clean before filming. On the night, only film guests who are happy to appear.
